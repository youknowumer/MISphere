Everything in one place

Built for the way MIS, IT and support teams actually work.

From the first task of the week to the report you send upward, every step lives in the same dashboard.

Office-hours timer

Every task counts real working time only. Set your office hours and working days once, and turnaround and efficiency are measured fairly.

Needs-attention panel

Overdue and due-tomorrow work surfaces on the home screen, sorted by deadline and priority, so nothing quietly slips.

Team scorecards and leader of the week

Transparent scoring from your own targets, member cards with progress, and a weekly leader history your team can see.

Excel in, Excel out

Import tasks from a styled template, export full workbooks with analytics sheets, or download a CSV weekly report.

Private by design

No accounts and no uploads. Data lives in your browser, with one-click JSON backup and restore whenever you want a copy.

Fast for daily use

A command palette (Ctrl or Cmd + K), keyboard shortcuts, light and dark themes, and a compact layout for busy desks.

Executive intelligence

Analytics that answer the questions leadership asks.

Not just counts. Flow metrics, forecasts and ranked recommendations show how fast work moves, how predictable delivery is, and what to fix first.

Throughput and stability
Weekly control chart with a normal range, so real change stands out from noise.
Turnaround percentiles
Median, P85 and P95 working days, ready to use as a service-level promise.
Backlog forecast
Projected clear date from recent pace, plus net flow to show if the backlog is growing.
Workload balance
An evenness index and per-member caps flag overload before deadlines are missed.
Estimate accuracy
Estimated versus actual hours per task, with bias and overrun rate.
Ranked priority actions
Critical, important and watch items generated from your own targets.

How it works

From empty workspace to management report in three steps.

01

Create your workspace

Pick a starting template, name your department and add your team. Targets and capacity limits are set for you and stay editable.

02

Log the work

Add tasks as they arrive, or import a week at once from the Excel template. Progress and the office-hours timer update as the team works.

03

Review and report

Open Insights for the executive summary, then print the report or export Excel and CSV for your weekly review.
